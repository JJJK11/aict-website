How It Works

The Certification Journey

Our structured, transparent certification process conforms to ISO/IEC 17021-1 requirements, ensuring every audit is thorough, fair, and defensible.

01

Application

Submit your application with details about your organization and management system scope.

02

Contract Review

We assess feasibility, define the audit scope, and establish a clear certification agreement.

03

Stage 1 Audit

Documentation review and readiness assessment to confirm your system is prepared for a full audit.

04

Stage 2 Audit

Comprehensive on-site or remote evaluation of your management system's implementation and effectiveness.

05

Certification Decision

An independent, impartial review of audit findings to determine if certification requirements are met.

06

Surveillance

Annual surveillance audits ensure ongoing conformity throughout the three-year certification cycle.

Why Choose AICT

Technical Expertise

Canadian Focus
