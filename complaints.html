Complaints & Appeals

We are committed to handling all complaints and appeals fairly, promptly, and transparently.

Overview

AICT Global Services Inc. maintains documented processes for receiving, evaluating, and resolving complaints and appeals in accordance with the requirements of ISO/IEC 17021-1. We treat every complaint and appeal seriously and are committed to resolving them in a fair, timely, and transparent manner.

Any person or organization may submit a complaint or appeal. There is no cost associated with filing a complaint. The complainant or appellant will be kept informed of the progress and outcome.

Complaints About AICT Global Services

A complaint about AICT relates to the activities, conduct, or services of AICT Global Services Inc. itself. This may include concerns about:

  • The conduct or competence of AICT personnel or contract auditors
  • The certification process or audit methodology
  • A certification decision made by AICT
  • Breaches of impartiality, confidentiality, or other policies
  • Any other aspect of AICT's operations or conduct

How to File

Complaints about AICT should be submitted in writing and should include sufficient detail to enable investigation, including:

  • The name and contact information of the complainant
  • A description of the issue, including relevant dates, people, and circumstances
  • Any supporting evidence or documentation
  • The outcome or resolution sought

Submit complaints to:
Email: complaints@aictglobalservices.com
Mail: AICT Global Services Inc., 162 Furry Creek Drive, Furry Creek, BC V8B 1A3, Canada
Subject line: Complaint — AICT

Complaints About a Certified Client

A complaint about a certified client relates to an organization that holds (or has held) a management system certificate issued by AICT. Such complaints may relate to:

  • Concerns about the certified client's conformity with the certified management system standard
  • Misuse or misrepresentation of the AICT certification mark or certificate
  • Claims that a certified organization is not operating in accordance with its certified management system

When AICT receives a complaint about a certified client, the complaint is investigated to determine whether it is relevant to the client's certification status. Where appropriate, the complaint is referred to the certified client and followed up during the next scheduled audit or through a special audit if warranted.

How to File

Complaints Process

The following outlines the general steps in AICT's complaints handling process:

1

Receipt and Acknowledgement

AICT acknowledges receipt of the complaint in writing within five (5) business days. The acknowledgement confirms that the complaint has been received and provides the name of the person responsible for handling it.

2

Validation

The complaint is reviewed to determine whether it falls within the scope of AICT's complaints process and whether sufficient information has been provided. If additional information is needed, the complainant is contacted.

3

Investigation

A competent person who was not involved in the subject of the complaint conducts an investigation. The investigation gathers relevant facts, reviews documentation, and may include interviews with relevant parties. Complaints about AICT personnel are investigated by individuals independent of the activity being complained about.

4

Resolution and Decision

Based on the investigation findings, AICT determines the appropriate resolution. This may include corrective action, changes to procedures, or other measures. The complainant is informed of the outcome and the basis for the decision.

5

Closure and Follow-Up

The complaint is formally closed once the resolution has been communicated and any corrective actions have been implemented and verified. Complaint records are retained and analyzed for trends as part of the management review process.

Target timeline: AICT aims to resolve complaints within thirty (30) business days of receipt. If a longer investigation period is required, the complainant will be notified of the revised timeline and the reasons for the delay.

Appeals

An appeal is a request by a client to reconsider a certification decision made by AICT. Certification decisions that may be appealed include decisions to grant, refuse, maintain, renew, suspend, restore, expand, reduce, or withdraw certification.

Grounds for Appeal

An appeal may be filed on the grounds that:

  • The certification decision was based on incorrect or incomplete information
  • The certification process was not conducted in accordance with AICT's documented procedures or the requirements of the applicable standard
  • Relevant evidence was not considered or was misinterpreted
  • There were procedural errors that materially affected the outcome

How to File an Appeal

Appeals must be submitted in writing within thirty (30) calendar days of the notification of the certification decision being appealed. The appeal should include:

  • The name and contact information of the appellant
  • Identification of the certification decision being appealed
  • The grounds for the appeal, with supporting evidence
  • The outcome sought

Appeals Process

  1. Acknowledgement: AICT acknowledges receipt of the appeal within five (5) business days.
  2. Review panel: An appeals panel is convened, consisting of competent persons who were not involved in the original certification decision. The panel reviews the appeal, the original audit evidence, and any additional information provided by the appellant.
  3. Hearing: The appellant is given the opportunity to present their case to the panel. This may be conducted in person, by video conference, or in writing, as agreed.
  4. Decision: The appeals panel makes a decision, which may uphold, modify, or overturn the original certification decision. The decision and its rationale are communicated to the appellant in writing.
  5. Implementation: If the appeal is upheld, AICT implements the revised decision and takes any necessary corrective actions.

Target timeline: AICT aims to resolve appeals within sixty (60) calendar days of receipt. If a longer period is required, the appellant will be notified.

Escalation

If the appellant is not satisfied with the outcome of the appeals process, the matter may be escalated to the accreditation body, the Standards Council of Canada (SCC). AICT will provide the appellant with SCC's contact information upon request.

Confidentiality

AICT treats all information received in connection with a complaint or appeal as confidential. The identity of the complainant or appellant will not be disclosed to the subject of the complaint without the consent of the complainant, except where disclosure is required by law or is necessary to resolve the matter.

All personnel involved in the handling of complaints and appeals are required to maintain confidentiality and to declare any conflicts of interest.
