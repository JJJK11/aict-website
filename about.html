About AICT Global Services

An independent Canadian certification body built on the principles of impartiality, technical excellence, and transparency.

Our Story

AICT Global Services Inc. was founded with a clear purpose: to provide Canadian and international organizations with credible, independent certification against the standards that define trust in a digital world. From information security to artificial intelligence governance, the landscape of conformity assessment is evolving rapidly, and organizations need a certification partner that understands both the technical depth of these standards and the regulatory context in which they operate.

Based in Vancouver, BC, Canada, AICT was established as an exclusively certification-focused body. We recognized that as AI regulation, data governance, and cybersecurity requirements grow more complex, organizations need a certification body that does not also sell consulting, implementation, or training services. Our independence is not a marketing claim. It is a structural commitment embedded in our governance, policies, and operations.

Mission

To provide impartial, competent, and consistent management system certification services that help organizations demonstrate conformity to internationally recognized and Canadian national standards, thereby contributing to trust, transparency, and accountability in the digital economy.

Vision

To be recognized as a trusted and technically authoritative certification body for emerging standards in information security, artificial intelligence, and digital governance, serving organizations across Canada and internationally with the highest standards of professionalism and integrity.

Our Principles

Core Values

Impartiality

Every certification decision is made without commercial pressure, conflict of interest, or external influence. Our Impartiality Committee provides independent oversight, and we maintain strict separation from consulting activities.

Technical Rigor

We invest in auditor competence and maintain deep expertise in the standards we certify against. Our audits assess the substance of management systems, not just documentation compliance.

Transparency

Our processes, policies, and fee structures are clearly documented and communicated. Organizations know exactly what to expect at every stage of the certification journey.

Accreditation Status

AICT Global Services is pursuing accreditation through the Standards Council of Canada (SCC), Canada's national accreditation body and a signatory to the International Accreditation Forum (IAF) Multilateral Recognition Arrangement (MLA).

Our management system has been designed and implemented in full conformity with ISO/IEC 17021-1:2015, the international standard specifying requirements for bodies providing audit and certification of management systems. This includes requirements for impartiality, competence, responsibility, openness, confidentiality, and responsiveness to complaints.

What this means for clients: AICT operates to the same rigorous requirements as accredited certification bodies worldwide. Our conformity with ISO/IEC 17021-1 means that our certification processes are systematic, evidence-based, and defensible. As we progress through the accreditation process, our certifications will carry the added assurance of SCC accreditation.

Quality Policy

AICT Global Services is committed to providing certification services of the highest quality. Our Quality Management System, documented in our Quality Manual (QM-001), establishes the framework for ensuring that:

  • All certification activities conform to ISO/IEC 17021-1:2015 requirements
  • Audit teams possess the required competence for each engagement
  • Certification decisions are made impartially and based solely on objective evidence
  • Our processes are continually improved through internal audit, management review, and corrective action
  • Confidentiality of client information is maintained at all times
  • Complaints and appeals are addressed promptly and fairly

Organizational Structure

AICT Global Services maintains a governance structure designed to ensure impartiality and competence in all certification activities:

  • Executive Management: Responsible for strategic direction, resource allocation, and overall management system effectiveness.
  • Impartiality Committee: An independent body with balanced representation from interested parties, responsible for overseeing the impartiality of certification activities. The Committee has the authority to influence certification decisions if impartiality is at risk.
  • Certification Decision Makers: Qualified individuals independent from the audit team who make final certification decisions based on audit evidence and findings.
  • Audit Teams: Composed of qualified lead auditors and technical experts with competence in the applicable standard and the client's technical area.
  • Quality Management: Responsible for maintaining the management system, conducting internal audits, managing corrective actions, and ensuring continual improvement.

Exclusively a Certification Body

AICT Global Services is exclusively a certification body. We do not provide management system consulting, implementation assistance, internal auditing for clients, training on management system implementation, or any other service that could compromise our impartiality.

This separation is absolute. It is required by ISO/IEC 17021-1, and it is a foundational commitment of our organization. If you need consulting services, we encourage you to engage an independent consultant. We are always happy to explain the certification process and requirements, but we cannot advise on how to design or implement your management system.

Canadian Identity

AICT is proudly based in Vancouver, BC, Canada. We understand the Canadian business environment, regulatory landscape, and the specific needs of Canadian organizations navigating both national standards (CAN/CIOSC) and international standards (ISO/IEC). We serve organizations across all provinces and territories, as well as international clients seeking certification against the standards we offer.
