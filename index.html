About AICT Global Services

Based in British Columbia, Canada, AICT Global Services Inc. is a certification body operating in conformity with ISO/IEC 17021-1. We provide management system certification audits to organizations seeking to demonstrate conformity with internationally recognized standards in information security, artificial intelligence, and data governance.

Our mission is to deliver rigorous, impartial, and competent certification services that build trust between our clients and their stakeholders.

Certification Services

We offer management system certification across four key standards in information security and AI governance.

ISO/IEC 27001

Information Security Management Systems

Demonstrate your organization's commitment to protecting information assets through a systematic approach to managing sensitive data. ISO/IEC 27001 is the world's leading standard for information security.

ISO/IEC 42001

Artificial Intelligence Management Systems

Establish a structured framework for the responsible development, deployment, and management of AI systems. ISO/IEC 42001 provides requirements for organizations using or providing AI-based products and services.

CAN/CIOSC 101

Ethical Design and Use of Automated Decision Systems

Meet Canadian national standards for the ethical design, development, and deployment of automated decision systems. Demonstrate algorithmic accountability and transparency to regulators and the public.

CAN/CIOSC 104

AI Data Governance

Ensure robust governance of data used in AI systems throughout its lifecycle. CAN/CIOSC 104 addresses data quality, provenance, bias mitigation, and responsible data management for artificial intelligence applications.
