About AICT Global Services

A Canadian certification body dedicated to rigorous, impartial, and competent assessment services.

Company Overview

AICT Global Services Inc. is a certification body headquartered in Furry Creek, British Columbia, Canada. We specialize in the assessment and certification of management systems related to information security, artificial intelligence, and data governance.

The name AICT reflects our core service areas: Assessment, Inspection, Certification, and Technology. We were established to address the growing need for competent, technology-focused certification services in Canada, particularly in the emerging fields of AI governance and automated decision systems.

AICT operates exclusively as a certification body. In accordance with the requirements of ISO/IEC 17021-1, we do not provide management system consulting services. This structural separation ensures the impartiality and integrity of every certification decision we make.

Our Mission

To provide independent, competent, and impartial management system certification services that enable organizations to demonstrate conformity with recognized standards, build stakeholder trust, and support the responsible adoption of technology.

We are committed to contributing to a marketplace where certification is meaningful, credible, and valued — both by the organizations we certify and by the stakeholders who rely on certification decisions.

Accreditation

Accreditation Status: AICT Global Services Inc. is currently in the process of seeking accreditation from the Standards Council of Canada (SCC) as a certification body operating in conformity with ISO/IEC 17021-1. This page will be updated once accreditation has been granted.

Accreditation by SCC provides independent confirmation that a certification body operates competently, impartially, and in conformity with the applicable international standards. SCC is Canada's national accreditation body and a signatory to the International Accreditation Forum (IAF) Multilateral Recognition Arrangement (MLA).

AICT's management system and certification processes have been designed and documented to meet the requirements of:

  • ISO/IEC 17021-1 — Conformity assessment — Requirements for bodies providing audit and certification of management systems
  • ISO/IEC 27006 — Requirements for bodies providing audit and certification of information security management systems (for ISO/IEC 27001 certification)
  • Applicable SCC requirements and procedures for accredited certification bodies

Commitment to Impartiality

Impartiality is fundamental to the credibility of certification. AICT Global Services is committed to ensuring that all certification activities are conducted impartially and that certification decisions are based solely on objective evidence of conformity.

We manage threats to impartiality through a systematic process that includes:

  • Identification and assessment of potential conflicts of interest before accepting any engagement
  • Strict prohibition on providing management system consulting services
  • An independent Impartiality Committee that provides oversight of our certification activities
  • Contractual and organizational safeguards to ensure auditor independence
  • A publicly accessible impartiality statement and complaint mechanism

For full details, see our Impartiality Statement.

Quality Policy

AICT Global Services Inc. is committed to delivering certification services that are:

  • Competent — Performed by qualified personnel with relevant technical expertise and maintained through ongoing professional development.
  • Impartial — Free from conflicts of interest, with certification decisions based solely on objective evidence.
  • Consistent — Delivered in accordance with documented procedures and the requirements of ISO/IEC 17021-1.
  • Transparent — Supported by clear communication with clients and publicly accessible information about our processes, policies, and certified clients.
  • Continually improving — Monitored through internal audits, management reviews, and feedback to identify and act on opportunities for improvement.

Top management is committed to ensuring the effectiveness of the management system and to providing the resources necessary to meet the needs of our clients and the requirements of our accreditation.
