About Certificate Verification

In accordance with the requirements of ISO/IEC 17021-1 (Clauses 8.1 and 8.2), AICT Global Services Inc. makes publicly available information about the current status of each management system certification it has granted. This directory enables any interested party to verify whether an organization holds a valid certificate issued by AICT.

The information provided includes the name of the certified organization, the scope of certification, the applicable standard, the certificate number, and the current status (active, suspended, or withdrawn).

Information Available in the Directory

Once certificates are issued, the following information will be publicly available for each certified organization:

  • Certificate number — Unique identifier for each certificate
  • Organization name — Legal name of the certified organization
  • Standard — The management system standard against which the organization is certified (e.g., ISO/IEC 27001, ISO/IEC 42001)
  • Scope of certification — Description of the activities, processes, and locations covered by the certificate
  • Issue date — Date the certificate was issued or most recently renewed
  • Expiry date — Date of certificate expiry (end of the three-year certification cycle)
  • Status — Current status of the certificate (Active, Suspended, or Withdrawn)

Note: Suspension of a certificate indicates a temporary interruption of certification, typically due to unresolved nonconformities. A suspended certificate may be restored once the issues are resolved, or withdrawn if they are not resolved within a specified timeframe. Withdrawal indicates that the certificate is no longer valid.

Reporting Misuse of Certification

If you believe an organization is misrepresenting its certification status or misusing a certificate or certification mark issued by AICT Global Services, please report the matter to us. Misuse of certification may include:

  • Claiming certification when no valid certificate exists
  • Displaying a suspended or withdrawn certificate as though it were active
  • Misrepresenting the scope, standard, or status of a certificate
  • Using the AICT certification mark in a misleading manner

Report misuse to: complaints@aictglobalservices.com with the subject line "Certification Misuse Report."
