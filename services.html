Certification Services

AICT Global Services provides management system certification against internationally recognized and Canadian national standards. Our audits are conducted in full conformity with ISO/IEC 17021-1:2015.

As an independent certification body, AICT evaluates whether an organization's management system conforms to the requirements of a chosen standard. We do not provide consulting services. Our role is strictly limited to impartial, evidence-based conformity assessment.

We currently offer certification against four standards, spanning information security, artificial intelligence management, ethical automated decision-making, and AI data governance. Each certification follows a rigorous, multi-stage audit process designed to provide confidence to your stakeholders, regulators, and customers.

ISO/IEC 27001:2022

Information Security Management Systems

ISO/IEC 27001 is the world's most recognized standard for information security management. It provides a systematic framework for managing sensitive company information, ensuring it remains secure through a combination of people, processes, and technology controls.

Who Needs This Certification?

Any organization that handles sensitive or confidential information benefits from ISO 27001 certification. This includes technology companies, financial services firms, healthcare organizations, government contractors, cloud service providers, and any business processing personal data or operating in regulated sectors.

What the Standard Covers

  • Context of the organization and interested party requirements
  • Leadership commitment and information security policy
  • Risk assessment and risk treatment processes
  • Operational security controls (Annex A controls)
  • Performance evaluation and internal audit
  • Continual improvement and management review

Benefits of Certification

Demonstrates due diligence in protecting information assets to clients, partners, and regulators

Reduces risk of data breaches and associated legal, financial, and reputational costs

Facilitates compliance with PIPEDA, GDPR, and sector-specific regulatory requirements

Often a prerequisite for government tenders, enterprise contracts, and supply chain requirements

ISO/IEC 42001:2023

Artificial Intelligence Management Systems

ISO/IEC 42001 is the first international standard dedicated to AI management systems. Published in 2023, it provides organizations with a structured framework for the responsible development, provision, and use of AI systems, addressing the unique risks and opportunities that artificial intelligence presents.

Why ISO 42001 Matters

As AI becomes embedded in business operations, the risks associated with bias, transparency, safety, and accountability grow. ISO 42001 provides an internationally agreed-upon framework for managing these risks systematically. With the EU AI Act and Canada's proposed AIDA legislation, proactive AI governance is no longer optional for forward-thinking organizations.

Who Needs This Certification?

  • Organizations developing or deploying AI and machine learning systems
  • Technology companies offering AI-powered products or services
  • Enterprises using AI for automated decision-making
  • Government departments and agencies deploying AI tools
  • Organizations seeking to demonstrate responsible AI practices to regulators and the public

Benefits of Certification

Demonstrates responsible AI governance to regulators, investors, and customers

Proactively addresses emerging regulatory requirements (EU AI Act, Canada AIDA)

Provides a systematic approach to managing AI-specific risks including bias, safety, and transparency

Competitive advantage as one of the first certified organizations in your sector

CAN/CIOSC 101:2019

Ethical Design and Use of Automated Decision Systems

CAN/CIOSC 101 is a Canadian national standard that provides a framework for the ethical design, development, and deployment of automated decision systems. It is particularly relevant to organizations whose automated systems affect the rights, entitlements, or interests of individuals.

Who Needs This Certification?

  • Government departments deploying automated decision tools (aligned with Treasury Board Directive on Automated Decision-Making)
  • Financial institutions using algorithmic credit scoring or risk assessment
  • Healthcare organizations using AI-assisted diagnostic or triage systems
  • Any organization whose automated systems produce decisions with significant impact on individuals

What the Standard Addresses

CAN/CIOSC 101 covers the full lifecycle of automated decision systems, from design through deployment and monitoring. It requires organizations to assess the impact level of their automated decisions, ensure appropriate human oversight, provide explanations of decisions to affected individuals, and establish processes for challenge and redress.

Benefits of Certification

Alignment with Canadian federal government expectations for automated decision systems

Demonstrates accountability and transparency in how automated decisions affect people

Mitigates legal and reputational risk from biased or opaque automated decisions

Provides a structured approach to ethical AI that builds public trust

CAN/CIOSC 104:2024

Responsible Governance of Data Used in Artificial Intelligence

CAN/CIOSC 104 addresses one of the most critical aspects of AI systems: the data that powers them. This Canadian national standard provides requirements and guidance for the responsible governance of data throughout its lifecycle when used in AI systems, from collection and preparation through use, retention, and disposal.

Who Needs This Certification?

  • Organizations training AI models on proprietary or public datasets
  • Data providers and aggregators supplying data for AI systems
  • Enterprises managing large-scale data pipelines for machine learning
  • Any organization seeking to ensure the quality, integrity, and ethical governance of AI training data

What the Standard Covers

CAN/CIOSC 104 establishes requirements for data provenance and lineage tracking, data quality assessment and improvement, bias identification and mitigation in datasets, consent and privacy in data collection, data security and access controls, and documentation and transparency throughout the data lifecycle.

Benefits of Certification

Ensures the integrity and quality of data feeding AI systems, reducing downstream risk

Demonstrates compliance with evolving data governance regulations and expectations

Pairs naturally with ISO 42001 certification for comprehensive AI governance

Addresses a critical gap in AI governance that most organizations have not yet formalized

The Process

Certification Process in Detail

Our certification process follows ISO/IEC 17021-1 requirements at every stage, ensuring consistency, objectivity, and defensibility.

  1. Application and Inquiry
    You submit an application providing information about your organization, the scope of your management system, the standard you wish to be certified against, the number of employees, and the nature of your operations. We use this to assess whether we can accept the engagement and to estimate audit time.
  2. Application Review and Contract
    AICT reviews your application to ensure we have the competence to audit your specific scope and that no conflicts of interest exist. We then issue a formal certification agreement that outlines the scope, timeline, audit team composition, and fee structure.
  3. Stage 1 Audit (Documentation Review)
    The Stage 1 audit evaluates your management system documentation for conformity with the standard's requirements. We assess your readiness for the Stage 2 audit, identify any areas of concern, and confirm the scope and logistics. This may be conducted remotely or on-site.
  4. Stage 2 Audit (Implementation Audit)
    The Stage 2 audit is a thorough, evidence-based assessment of your management system's implementation and effectiveness. Our auditors evaluate conformity through interviews, observation, document review, and sampling. Nonconformities are documented with clear, evidence-based findings.
  5. Certification Decision
    Following the Stage 2 audit, an independent certification decision-maker reviews all audit evidence and findings. This person was not involved in the audit itself, ensuring an impartial certification decision. Certification is granted only when all requirements are demonstrably met.
  6. Certificate Issuance
    Upon a positive decision, AICT issues a certificate specifying the standard, scope, effective date, and expiration date. The certificate is valid for three years, subject to successful annual surveillance audits.
  7. Surveillance Audits
    Annual surveillance audits verify ongoing conformity. These are less extensive than the initial certification audit but cover key areas and any previously identified concerns. The first surveillance must occur within 12 months of the initial certification decision.
  8. Recertification
    Before the three-year certification cycle expires, a recertification audit evaluates the continued effectiveness of the entire management system. Successful recertification begins a new three-year cycle.
Audit Methodology

How We Conduct Audits

AICT conducts audits using methods appropriate to your organization and management system. We offer flexible delivery while maintaining the rigor and thoroughness that certification demands.

On-Site Audits

Traditional on-site audits involve our audit team visiting your facilities to conduct interviews, observe operations, and review documentation in person. On-site audits are required where physical processes, infrastructure, or environmental factors are integral to your management system's scope.

Remote Audits

Where appropriate and in accordance with IAF MD 4 requirements, we conduct audits remotely using secure communication technologies. Remote auditing is effective for organizations with digital operations, cloud-based systems, and distributed workforces. We ensure that remote methods maintain the same evidentiary rigor as on-site audits.

Hybrid Audits

Many audits combine on-site and remote methods. For example, document reviews and management interviews may be conducted remotely, while operational observations occur on-site. We tailor the approach to your specific context.

Audit Team Competence: Every audit is conducted by qualified auditors with demonstrated competence in the applicable standard and relevant technical expertise in your organization's sector. Audit team composition is determined during the application review stage.
