Impartiality Commitment

Impartiality is the foundation of credible certification. AICT Global Services is structurally and operationally committed to ensuring that every certification decision is objective, independent, and free from undue influence.

Our Commitment

AICT Global Services Inc. is committed to impartiality in all certification activities. We recognize that impartiality, and the perception of impartiality, is essential to delivering certification that stakeholders can trust. This commitment is embedded in our governance structure, management system, operational procedures, and the conduct of every person acting on behalf of AICT.

Our impartiality commitment is not a formality. It is a binding obligation documented in our Quality Manual (QM-001) and enforced through our Impartiality Committee, internal audits, and management review process. All AICT personnel, contractors, and committee members are required to sign declarations of impartiality and disclose any potential conflicts of interest.

How We Ensure Impartiality

AICT employs a comprehensive set of structural and procedural safeguards to protect the impartiality of our certification activities:

1. Prohibition on Consulting

AICT does not provide management system consulting, implementation assistance, internal auditing for clients, or training on management system design. This prohibition is absolute and applies to all AICT personnel, contractors, and related entities. We do not offer, directly or through related entities, any service that could compromise the independence of our certification decisions.

2. Conflict of Interest Screening

Before every audit engagement, AICT conducts a thorough conflict of interest assessment. This includes evaluating whether any member of the audit team, the certification decision maker, or any AICT personnel has a financial, personal, professional, or other relationship with the applicant or certified client that could introduce bias.

3. Separation of Audit and Decision

The person or persons who make the certification decision are not the same individuals who conducted the audit. This separation ensures that certification decisions are based on an independent review of audit evidence and findings, free from any influence that may arise during the audit process itself.

4. Personnel Rotation

AICT implements audit team rotation policies to prevent familiarity threats that can develop when the same auditors repeatedly assess the same organization over multiple certification cycles.

5. Impartiality Declarations

All AICT personnel, contractors, auditors, and committee members are required to sign impartiality and confidentiality declarations. These declarations are reviewed and renewed periodically, and any changes in circumstances that could affect impartiality must be reported immediately.

6. Financial Independence

AICT's fee structure is designed so that no single client or group of clients represents a disproportionate share of revenue. Certification fees are based on objective criteria (scope, size, complexity) and are determined before the audit begins. Audit outcomes do not affect fees, and auditors are not compensated based on certification outcomes.

7. Organizational Risk Assessment

AICT conducts ongoing risk assessments to identify threats to impartiality, including self-interest threats, self-review threats, familiarity threats, and intimidation threats. For each identified threat, appropriate safeguards are implemented and documented. This risk assessment is reviewed during management review and whenever significant changes occur.

The Impartiality Committee

AICT's Impartiality Committee is an independent governance body composed of representatives from key interested parties, including but not limited to:

  • Certified client representatives
  • Industry or sector representatives
  • Regulatory or government representatives
  • Consumer or public interest representatives

The Impartiality Committee does not make certification decisions, but it has significant authority and responsibility:

  • Reviewing AICT's policies and procedures to ensure they adequately protect impartiality
  • Monitoring certification activities for any threats to impartiality
  • Advising AICT management on impartiality matters
  • Authority to require AICT to take corrective action if impartiality is threatened
  • Reviewing the outcome of complaints and appeals related to impartiality
  • Access to all information necessary to fulfil its oversight role

Committee Authority: The Impartiality Committee has the authority to influence AICT's certification activities if it determines that impartiality is at risk. AICT management is obligated to respond to the Committee's recommendations and to document the actions taken.

Prohibition on Consulting

To reiterate this critical point: AICT Global Services does not provide, and has never provided, consulting services of any kind to organizations that it certifies or may certify. This includes:

  • Management system design or implementation consulting
  • Internal audit services for clients
  • Training services on management system implementation
  • Risk assessment or gap analysis as a precursor to certification
  • Any other service that would constitute providing management system advice

AICT will explain certification requirements and provide information about the certification process, but we will not advise on how to meet those requirements. The distinction between informing and advising is rigorously maintained.

Related Entities Disclosure

AICT Global Services Inc. does not have any related entities that provide management system consulting, training, or implementation services. Should this status change at any time, it will be disclosed here and reported to the Impartiality Committee and relevant accreditation bodies. Any such relationship would be subject to formal risk assessment and safeguards in accordance with ISO/IEC 17021-1.

Reporting Impartiality Concerns

Any person who has reason to believe that AICT's impartiality may be compromised is encouraged and invited to report their concern. Reports can be made by:

  • Certified clients or applicant organizations
  • Users of certification (purchasers, regulators, consumers)
  • AICT personnel, contractors, or auditors
  • Members of the public

All impartiality concerns are treated confidentially and investigated thoroughly. Reports will not result in any adverse consequences for the reporter.

Contact for Impartiality Matters: Please direct impartiality concerns to impartiality@aictglobalservices.com or to the Impartiality Committee through info@aictglobalservices.com.
