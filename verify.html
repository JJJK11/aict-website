About the Certificate Directory

In accordance with ISO/IEC 17021-1:2015 requirements for openness, AICT maintains a publicly accessible directory of current certifications. This directory enables interested parties, including purchasers, regulators, consumers, and the general public, to verify the certification status of organizations certified by AICT.

Information Available in the Directory

For each certified organization, the following information is publicly available:

  • Name and address of the certified organization
  • Dates of granting, extending, or renewing certification
  • The standard(s) against which certification has been granted
  • The scope of certification
  • Current certification status (active, suspended, withdrawn)
  • Certificate number

Certificate Contents

Certificates issued by AICT Global Services contain the following information:

  • The name and location of the certified organization
  • The effective date and expiration date of the certificate
  • The standard and version certified against
  • The scope of the certified management system
  • A unique certificate identification number
  • The name and contact information of AICT Global Services as the issuing certification body
  • Accreditation mark (where applicable)

Reporting Suspected Fraudulent Certificates

If you have reason to believe that a certificate claiming to be issued by AICT Global Services is fraudulent, altered, or otherwise invalid, please report it to us immediately. Fraudulent use of certification documents undermines the trust that certification is designed to provide, and AICT takes all such reports seriously.

Report suspected fraudulent certificates to: info@aictglobalservices.com
Please include a copy of the suspected document and any relevant context.

Misuse of Certification Marks

Certified organizations are authorized to use AICT's certification marks in accordance with the terms specified in their certification agreement. Misuse of certification marks includes:

  • Using the mark after certification has been suspended or withdrawn
  • Using the mark in a misleading manner, such as implying certification covers products or services outside the certified scope
  • Altering the mark or using it in a way that misrepresents its meaning
  • Use by organizations that have never held valid certification from AICT

If you are aware of any misuse of AICT's certification marks, please report it to info@aictglobalservices.com. AICT will investigate and take appropriate action, which may include requiring corrective action, suspending or withdrawing certification, and pursuing legal remedies.
