Privacy Policy

How AICT Global Services Inc. collects, uses, discloses, and protects personal information.

Effective Date: January 1, 2026  |  Last Updated: February 2026

Introduction

AICT Global Services Inc. ("AICT", "we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting the privacy of personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect personal information in connection with our certification services and website operations.

AICT operates in compliance with the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and applicable Vancouver, BC privacy legislation, including the Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA).

Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of personal information:

Through Our Website

  • Name, email address, organization name, and message content when you submit a contact form
  • Technical data such as IP address, browser type, and pages visited (collected automatically through standard web server logs)

Through Certification Activities

  • Names, roles, and contact information of personnel involved in certified management systems
  • Professional qualifications and competence information of auditors and technical experts
  • Information contained in management system documentation reviewed during audits
  • Audit evidence and findings, which may include references to individuals
  • Complaint and appeal information, including the identity of complainants and appellants

How We Use Personal Information

We use personal information for the following purposes:

  • Responding to inquiries and contact form submissions
  • Processing certification applications and conducting audits
  • Making certification decisions
  • Maintaining the public certificate directory (limited to organization name, certification scope, dates, and status)
  • Processing complaints and appeals
  • Maintaining auditor and personnel competence records
  • Meeting legal and regulatory requirements, including accreditation requirements
  • Improving our website and services

Disclosure of Personal Information

AICT does not sell, rent, or trade personal information. We may disclose personal information in the following limited circumstances:

  • Accreditation bodies: The Standards Council of Canada or other accreditation bodies may review certification records as part of their oversight of AICT's operations.
  • Public certificate directory: Organization-level information (not personal information of individuals) is published in our public directory as required by ISO/IEC 17021-1.
  • Legal requirements: Where required by law, court order, or regulatory authority.
  • Consent: Where the individual has provided explicit consent.

Confidentiality in Certification

AICT maintains strict confidentiality regarding all information obtained during the certification process. This is a core requirement of ISO/IEC 17021-1. All AICT personnel, auditors, contractors, and committee members are bound by confidentiality agreements. Client-specific information is never disclosed to other clients or to the public, except as described in this policy and the certification agreement.

Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These measures include access controls, encryption of sensitive data in transit, secure storage of records, and regular review of security practices.

Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, including to meet legal, regulatory, and accreditation record-keeping requirements. Certification records are retained for a minimum period following the end of the certification cycle, as required by ISO/IEC 17021-1 and accreditation requirements.

Your Rights

Under Canadian privacy law, you have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate personal information
  • Withdraw consent for the use of your personal information (subject to legal and contractual limitations)
  • File a complaint about our privacy practices

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the information below.

Cookies and Website Analytics

Our website may use essential cookies necessary for the website to function. We do not use tracking cookies or third-party advertising technologies. If we implement analytics in the future, this policy will be updated accordingly, and users will be notified.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
