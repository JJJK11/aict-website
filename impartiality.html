Impartiality Statement

Our commitment to independent, objective, and conflict-free certification.

Commitment to Impartiality

AICT Global Services Inc. recognizes that impartiality is essential to the credibility and value of management system certification. The trust placed in certification by clients, regulators, and the public depends on certification decisions being made objectively, based solely on evidence of conformity, and free from any undue influence.

AICT's top management is committed to impartiality in all certification activities. This commitment is communicated to all personnel, contractors, and committees involved in certification and is embedded in our policies, procedures, and organizational structure.

Public Commitment: AICT Global Services Inc. commits to acting impartially and ensuring that certification decisions are based on objective evidence of conformity or nonconformity. Certification will not be granted, maintained, renewed, expanded, reduced, suspended, or withdrawn on any basis other than the results of the certification process.

Managing Conflicts of Interest

AICT has established and maintains a systematic process for identifying, analyzing, evaluating, treating, monitoring, and documenting threats to impartiality. This process is applied at the organizational level and for each individual certification engagement.

Sources of Threats to Impartiality

Threats to impartiality may arise from, but are not limited to:

  • Self-interest threats — Financial or other interests that could influence certification decisions
  • Self-review threats — Auditing work previously performed by the same individual or organization
  • Familiarity or trust threats — Close relationships between audit personnel and the client organization
  • Intimidation threats — Pressure or coercion from a client or other party
  • Commercial or competitive threats — Financial dependence on a single client or pressure to compete on the basis of leniency

Safeguards

AICT employs the following safeguards to manage threats to impartiality:

  • No consulting services: AICT does not provide management system consulting services. This prohibition extends to all personnel, contractors, and any entity with a relationship to AICT that could compromise impartiality. This is a fundamental requirement of ISO/IEC 17021-1.
  • Conflict of interest review: Prior to accepting any certification engagement, AICT conducts a conflict of interest assessment covering the organization, its related entities, and all proposed audit team members.
  • Personnel declarations: All personnel involved in certification activities are required to declare any interests, relationships, or circumstances that could present a conflict of interest. These declarations are reviewed and updated regularly.
  • Audit team rotation: Audit team leaders are rotated at appropriate intervals to mitigate familiarity threats.
  • Separation of audit and decision-making: Certification decisions are made by competent persons who were not involved in the conduct of the audit, ensuring an independent review of audit findings and recommendations.
  • Financial safeguards: AICT monitors the proportion of income derived from any single client to ensure that financial dependence does not create a threat to impartiality.
  • Cooling-off periods: AICT observes appropriate cooling-off periods before engaging individuals who have previously provided consulting or other potentially conflicting services to a client organization.

Impartiality Committee

In accordance with the requirements of ISO/IEC 17021-1, AICT has established an Impartiality Committee to provide oversight of its certification activities and to safeguard impartiality.

Role and Responsibilities

The Impartiality Committee is responsible for:

  • Reviewing and providing input on AICT's policies and procedures related to impartiality
  • Monitoring identified threats to impartiality and the effectiveness of safeguards
  • Advising top management on matters affecting impartiality, including commercial, financial, and relationship-related risks
  • Reviewing any situations where impartiality may have been or may appear to have been compromised
  • Providing a balanced representation of stakeholder interests

Composition

The Impartiality Committee is composed of members representing a balance of interested parties. No single interest predominates. The committee includes representation from parties such as:

  • Certified clients or client representatives
  • Industry or sector stakeholders
  • Regulatory or governmental bodies
  • Users of certification or other interested parties

AICT ensures that the composition of the Impartiality Committee provides sufficient independence from the commercial interests of the certification body.

Authority

The Impartiality Committee has the authority to raise concerns directly with AICT's top management and, if necessary, with the accreditation body (SCC). AICT takes all concerns raised by the Impartiality Committee seriously and documents actions taken in response.

Reporting Impartiality Concerns

Any person or organization may raise a concern about the impartiality of AICT's certification activities. AICT takes all such concerns seriously and investigates them promptly and thoroughly.

Concerns may relate to, but are not limited to:

  • Perceived or actual conflicts of interest involving AICT personnel or contractors
  • Situations where a certification decision appears to have been influenced by factors other than objective evidence
  • Relationships between AICT and a certified client that could compromise objectivity
  • Any other matter that could undermine confidence in the impartiality of AICT's certification services

How to Report

Impartiality concerns may be submitted in writing to:

Email: complaints@aictglobalservices.com
Mail: AICT Global Services Inc., 162 Furry Creek Drive, Furry Creek, BC V8B 1A3, Canada
Subject line: Impartiality Concern

All impartiality concerns are reviewed by the Quality Manager and, where appropriate, referred to the Impartiality Committee. The person raising the concern will receive acknowledgement of receipt and will be informed of the outcome of the investigation, subject to confidentiality requirements.

Related Entities Disclosure

In accordance with the requirements of ISO/IEC 17021-1, AICT discloses any relationships with related entities that could present a threat to impartiality.

AICT Global Services Inc. operates exclusively as a certification body. We maintain structural, organizational, and financial separation from any entity that provides management system consulting, training, or other services that could create a self-review or other impartiality threat.

AICT does not share management, personnel, or resources with any consulting organization. Where a related entity is identified, AICT assesses the threat to impartiality and applies appropriate safeguards. Any such relationships and the safeguards applied are disclosed to the Impartiality Committee and, where required, to the accreditation body.

Disclosure: As of the date of this statement, AICT Global Services Inc. does not have any related entities that provide management system consulting services within the scopes of certification offered by AICT.

Review and Updates

This impartiality statement is reviewed at least annually as part of AICT's management review process and is updated as necessary to reflect changes in organizational relationships, certification activities, or threats to impartiality. The most recent review was conducted by top management.

For questions about this statement or AICT's impartiality policies, please contact us at info@aictglobalservices.com.
