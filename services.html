Certification Services

Independent, accredited management system certification audits in information security, AI management, and AI governance.

ISO/IEC 27001

Information Security Management Systems

ISO/IEC 27001 is the internationally recognized standard for establishing, implementing, maintaining, and continually improving an information security management system (ISMS). Certification to ISO/IEC 27001 demonstrates to customers, regulators, and stakeholders that your organization takes a systematic approach to managing sensitive information.

Who Should Seek Certification?

  • Organizations that process, store, or transmit sensitive or regulated data
  • Technology companies and service providers handling client information
  • Organizations subject to contractual or regulatory information security requirements
  • Government suppliers and contractors requiring security certifications

Key Benefits

  • Demonstrate a structured approach to information security risk management
  • Meet regulatory and contractual security requirements
  • Build trust with clients, partners, and stakeholders
  • Reduce the likelihood and impact of security incidents
  • Gain competitive advantage in markets where security assurance is valued
ISO/IEC 42001

Artificial Intelligence Management Systems

ISO/IEC 42001 specifies requirements for establishing, implementing, maintaining, and continually improving an AI management system (AIMS) within organizations that provide or use AI-based products or services. It is the first international management system standard dedicated to artificial intelligence.

Who Should Seek Certification?

  • Organizations developing, deploying, or operating AI-based systems
  • Technology companies offering AI products or services
  • Organizations using AI to make or support decisions affecting individuals
  • Companies seeking to demonstrate responsible AI practices to regulators

Key Benefits

  • Establish governance over AI systems throughout their lifecycle
  • Demonstrate responsible and trustworthy AI practices
  • Address regulatory expectations for AI accountability and transparency
  • Manage risks associated with AI development and deployment
  • Build stakeholder confidence in AI-driven products and services
CAN/CIOSC 101

Ethical Design and Use of Automated Decision Systems

CAN/CIOSC 101 is a Canadian national standard addressing the ethical design, development, and deployment of automated decision systems (ADS). It provides a framework for algorithmic accountability, fairness assessment, and transparency in automated decision-making processes.

Who Should Seek Certification?

  • Public sector organizations using automated systems for decisions affecting individuals
  • Private sector companies deploying algorithmic decision-making tools
  • Organizations subject to Canada's Directive on Automated Decision-Making
  • Entities seeking to demonstrate ethical AI governance to Canadian regulators

Key Benefits

  • Demonstrate compliance with Canadian standards for automated decision-making
  • Ensure fairness, transparency, and accountability in algorithmic systems
  • Mitigate legal and reputational risks associated with biased or opaque decisions
  • Build public trust in automated processes
CAN/CIOSC 104

AI Data Governance

CAN/CIOSC 104 establishes requirements for the governance of data used in artificial intelligence systems. It addresses the full data lifecycle — from collection and preparation through to usage, storage, and disposal — with a focus on data quality, provenance, bias mitigation, and responsible management.

Who Should Seek Certification?

  • Organizations managing data pipelines that feed AI and machine learning systems
  • Data-intensive organizations seeking governance frameworks for AI data
  • Companies aiming to demonstrate robust data practices to regulators and clients
  • AI system developers needing to ensure data quality and traceability

Key Benefits

  • Establish structured governance over AI training and operational data
  • Mitigate bias and quality issues arising from poorly governed data
  • Demonstrate data provenance and traceability for regulatory compliance
  • Enhance stakeholder confidence in AI outputs through sound data practices

The Certification Process

Our certification process follows the requirements of ISO/IEC 17021-1 and consists of the following stages.

1

Application and Review

Submit a certification application providing details about your organization, scope of the management system, and the standard(s) you wish to be certified against. AICT reviews the application to confirm we have the competence and capacity to perform the audit, and that no conflicts of interest exist.

2

Stage 1 Audit

The Stage 1 audit evaluates your organization's readiness for the Stage 2 audit. This includes a review of management system documentation, understanding of your organization's context and processes, evaluation of internal audit and management review results, and confirmation of scope and audit planning.

3

Stage 2 Audit

The Stage 2 audit is a comprehensive, on-site (or remote, where applicable) assessment of the implementation and effectiveness of your management system. Auditors evaluate conformity with all requirements of the applicable standard, gather objective evidence, and identify any nonconformities.

4

Certification Decision

Following the Stage 2 audit and resolution of any nonconformities, an independent certification decision is made by a competent person or committee not involved in the audit. If the decision is positive, a certificate of conformity is issued for a three-year certification cycle.

5

Surveillance Audits

Surveillance audits are conducted at planned intervals (at least annually) throughout the three-year certification cycle. These audits confirm the continued conformity and effectiveness of the management system and address any changes since the previous audit.

6

Recertification

Prior to the expiry of the three-year certification cycle, a recertification audit is conducted to evaluate the continued conformity and effectiveness of the management system as a whole. Upon a positive decision, a new three-year certification cycle begins.
